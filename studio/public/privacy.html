TOME Studio

Privacy Policy

For TOME on Android and TOME Studio (tome.rahulkannan.com). Last updated 29 October 2026.

In short: TOME doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your device.

TOME (the app)

  • On your device: the map packs you add, which markers you've checked off, your filter choices, any markers you edit and any photos you add to them. TOME never sends any of this to me or anyone else.
  • Photos: when you add a photo to a marker, you take it with your camera or choose it from your phone, and TOME keeps a smaller copy with your markers. It isn't shared unless you choose Export markers yourself.
  • Your location: on maps of real places, you can turn on a "you are here" dot. Only then does TOME ask for location permission, and it uses your location only while that map is open on your screen, to draw the dot (or the arrow and distance when you're off the map). Your location is never saved, never sent anywhere and never used in the background. You can turn it off at any time, or remove the permission in your phone's settings.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android saves your progress, filter choices, markers you've added or edited, and the list of packs you've added (with their links) to your Google account, the same way it backs up other apps, so they come back on a new phone. The packs themselves aren't included; they download again from their links. Photos you've added aren't included either (the backup has a size limit), but moving to a new phone with a cable or Android's phone-to-phone transfer brings them along. Your location is never backed up, since it's never saved. This backup is handled by Android and Google, and I can't see it. You can turn it off in Settings > System > Backup. Uninstalling TOME deletes everything on the phone; Google removes old backups by its own schedule.
  • Downloading packs: when you add or update a pack, the app downloads it directly from the GitHub address you give it. GitHub receives that request (like any website visit, including your IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement. The app sends nothing else.
  • Links you tap (wiki pages, the website, Buy Me a Coffee) open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own policies.
  • Permissions: internet access, used only to download packs, and location, asked for only when you turn on "you are here" and used only as described above. Taking or choosing a photo uses Android's own camera and photo picker, so TOME doesn't need access to your camera or your photo library.

TOME Studio (the website)

  • Your work (pack details, markers, layouts, small icon images) is saved in your browser's local storage on your computer. Map images stay in the page and are never uploaded. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Opening a published pack fetches its files from GitHub, and its map tiles through a small relay on Cloudflare, which passes files through without storing anything about you.
  • No cookies are used for tracking or advertising.

Children

TOME isn't aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
